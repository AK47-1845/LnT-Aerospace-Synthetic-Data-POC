Aerospace Structural Data Synthesis — Final Report

This program benchmarked six tabular generative approaches for aerospace structural design synthesis across 72 controlled experiments. The goal was to identify the safest and most useful synthetic data strategy for client-facing downstream modeling.

0.9969Best Score

Executive Summary

The evaluation compared Empirical, KDE, GMM, Copula, TabuDiff, and MaskedPredictor generators over three seeds and four generation sizes. The strongest individual run was Empirical with seed 11 at size 5000, reaching a composite score of 0.9969. KDE posted the strongest average score across all of its runs, but Empirical delivered the cleanest champion configuration and the simplest operational profile. The recommended deployment path is Empirical or KDE at generation size 1000 or higher, with Empirical as the default client-ready choice.

  1. Empirical achieved the top single-run result while staying nearly perfect on physics, statistics, privacy, and utility.
  2. KDE is a legitimate production-grade backup because its mean performance is slightly stronger than Empirical across all 12 runs.
  3. Classical parametric models preserved engineering realism but lost too much downstream ML utility relative to the top tier.
  4. MaskedPredictor requires a major architectural rethink before further client use.

Model Verdicts

CHAMPION

Empirical

Empirical resampling delivered the single best configuration in the study and stayed almost perfectly inside the real engineering envelope.

It is the safest production choice when fidelity and deployment simplicity matter more than novelty in sampling behavior.

Mean Composite: 0.9631
STRONG

KDE

KDE matched Empirical on most axes and posted the strongest average composite score across all 12 runs.

It is the best alternative when a smoother non-parametric generator is preferred without giving up utility.

Mean Composite: 0.9650
MODERATE

GMM

GMM preserved physics and broad statistics well, but the downstream ML utility remained materially weaker than the top two models.

It is acceptable for exploratory baselines, not for high-fidelity production synthesis.

Mean Composite: 0.8345
MODERATE

Copula

Copula kept structural realism reasonably high but lagged on ML transfer performance and overall composite score.

It works as a transparent classical benchmark, though not as a front-runner for deployment.

Mean Composite: 0.8124
MODERATE

TabuDiff

TabuDiff showed respectable physics preservation but paid a quality penalty in statistical fit and practical utility.

It needs further tuning before it can compete with simpler baselines on this dataset.

Mean Composite: 0.7965
WEAK

MaskedPredictor

MaskedPredictor underperformed sharply, with near-zero statistical similarity and very weak privacy behavior in the logged results.

It should not be used for production until the architecture is substantially redesigned.

Mean Composite: 0.4313

Recommendations & Conclusions

  1. Use Empirical or KDE at size ≥ 1000 for production-oriented aerospace synthesis.
  2. Prefer Empirical when stakeholder trust, traceability, and fastest deployment matter most.
  3. Do not deploy MaskedPredictor without architecture overhaul and renewed benchmarking.
  4. Future work should explore ensemble generators and conditional synthesis for design-target steering.
