Conclusion

Use Empirical when you need the strongest proven single-run fidelity with the least operational complexity. Use KDE when you want nearly identical quality with a smoother sampling model and still-low compute cost. Keep GMM and Copula for classical baselines, and reserve TabuDiff or MaskedPredictor for future research rather than near-term delivery.
