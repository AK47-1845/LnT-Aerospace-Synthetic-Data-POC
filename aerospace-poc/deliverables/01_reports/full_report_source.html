Executive Summary

This report summarizes 72 recorded synthesis experiments across six model families for aerospace structural design data. The best observed run was Empirical with seed 11 and size 5000, reaching a composite score of 0.9969. KDE produced the strongest average score across all of its runs, but Empirical supplied the single best client-ready configuration. The recommended operational policy is to use Empirical or KDE at size 1000 or above.

Methodology

The pipeline profiled the original 300-row, 22-feature aerospace dataset, trained six model families over three seeds and four generation scales, evaluated four quality pillars, and then ranked every run using the composite score. This deliverable package was assembled strictly from those recorded artifacts without rerunning the experiment suite.
